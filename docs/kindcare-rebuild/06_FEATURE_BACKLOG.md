# KindCare Feature Backlog

These are strong additions after the core release. Do not build them all at once; prioritize features that deepen connection and reduce caregiver burden without making the member experience busy or clinical.

## Priority A — High value soon after launch

### Daily wellbeing check-in

The member can choose “I’m doing well,” “I’m okay,” or “I’d like to talk,” plus an optional note. This is a communication prompt, not a health assessment or medical monitoring tool.

### One-tap requests

Large, customizable buttons such as “Please call me,” “I need groceries,” “I need a ride,” and “I need help with something.” A caregiver can see open requests and mark them resolved.

### Family bulletin board and moments

Approved household participants can post warm updates, photos, birthday messages, and short notes. The member sees a calm, chronological “Moments” view without public/social-network features.

### Caregiver handoff

For households with multiple helpers: a short secure “What changed today?” note, assignments, and unread acknowledgments. Keep it distinct from clinical documentation.

### Appointment preparation

For each appointment: shared questions, documents to bring, transport plan, and follow-up tasks. Do not create clinical summaries or recommendations.

## Priority B — Helpful coordination upgrades

### Transportation coordination

Ride request, volunteer/driver assignment, pickup time, contact details, and “arrived safely” confirmation.

### Refill and household supply reminders

Caregiver-entered reminders to contact a pharmacy or replenish household items. No prescription ordering or medication recommendations in the first version.

### Weekly digest

A caregiver-selected weekly email summary through Resend: upcoming appointments, outstanding tasks, recent voice notes, and unresolved requests. Make it opt-in and avoid including private details in an email subject line.

### Printable household card

An accessible printout containing trusted contacts, upcoming appointments, and a caregiver-maintained medication list. Encourage the family to verify it against prescription labels.

## Priority C — Accessibility and delight

- Large-text and high-contrast modes
- Reading level / simplified-language preference
- Voice-note transcript and adjustable playback speed
- Preferred language support
- “Good morning” / “good evening” greeting and a gentle photo of the day
- Member-selectable quiet hours and notification preferences

## Features to defer or avoid

- Passive monitoring, location tracking, fall detection, or surveillance-style alerts
- AI medical assessment, dosage advice, diagnosis, or drug-interaction recommendations
- Public feeds, ads, or selling/sharing household data
- Automatically sending emergency services
