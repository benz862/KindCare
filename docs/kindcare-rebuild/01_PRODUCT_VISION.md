# KindCare Product Vision

## Purpose

KindCare gives families a better way to stay close when everyday support is needed. It should reduce the emotional and practical friction of checking in, remembering small care tasks, and keeping a loved one connected.

The product is not a clinical record system. It is not a surveillance product. It is not an emergency-response service. It is a private family coordination and connection space.

## Primary people

### 1. Caregiver / household organizer

Often an adult child, spouse, or trusted friend. They are busy, may be worried, and need a quick view of what matters today. They create the household, manage trusted people, send notes, schedule supportive routines, and respond to requests.

### 2. Member / loved one

Often an older adult who may be less comfortable with technology. Their experience needs large controls, plain language, no dense dashboards, and a clear sense that the app is there for them—not watching them.

### 3. Trusted helper

A sibling, neighbor, friend, or professional helper who is invited to take part in specific, appropriate ways. They only see what their role permits.

## Product principles

1. **Connection before administration.** A warm voice note matters as much as a scheduled item.
2. **Gentle, not alarming.** Use friendly language and avoid red warning-heavy interfaces.
3. **Simple by default.** The member home screen should have only the few actions they need today.
4. **Consent is visible.** Members should understand who is included and what is shared.
5. **Privacy by design.** Household boundaries and permissions are enforced in the database, not merely hidden in the interface.
6. **Useful in under a minute.** A caregiver can leave a note or add a reminder quickly; a member can listen or request help in one tap.

## First-release capabilities

### Caregiver workspace

- Create a private household and invite members/helpers.
- See a calm “Today” view: upcoming routines, recent messages, open requests, and last connection.
- Record or upload a short voice note; optionally include a short text summary.
- Schedule a voice note to arrive on a chosen day and time, once or on a repeating cadence.
- Schedule supportive routines: check-in, appointment reminder, medication reminder, or custom item.
- Assign a routine to a member, with a simple completion/check-in action.
- Maintain a shared household calendar for appointments, events, reminders, and scheduled voice notes.
- Create a caregiver-entered medication plan: medicine name, label strength, amount, timing, optional instructions, and active dates.
- See whether each planned dose was marked taken, skipped, or needs follow-up.
- Maintain trusted contacts and configurable emergency contacts.
- Review member requests and household activity.

### Member home

- Large “Listen to today’s message” card.
- Large “Talk to someone” button that shows trusted contacts.
- A prominent bulletin area for today’s messages, reminders, and upcoming events.
- A short list of today’s medication doses and tasks, with simple “Taken,” “Done,” “Skip,” and “I need help” choices where appropriate.
- A simplified calendar view that shows appointments and important events.
- A prominent “I need help” action.
- An accessible settings/privacy screen with text size, trusted contacts, and sharing explanation.

### Help action safety behavior

- Before activation, caregivers configure trusted contacts and the action’s behavior.
- The member sees: “For an immediate emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.”
- The action can call/text configured trusted contacts and create an in-app alert; it must never imply that KindCare dispatches help or monitors emergencies.
- Require a clear confirmation step unless the household has deliberately chosen a one-tap workflow.

## Not in version one

- Clinical diagnosis or medical advice
- Automatic fall detection, passive monitoring, or location tracking
- Insurance workflows or electronic health records
- A public social feed
- AI-generated medical recommendations
- Complex multi-household administration

## Medication safety boundary

KindCare may help a household **record and remember** a medication plan. It does not prescribe medicine, calculate doses, check interactions, verify that a medicine was swallowed, or replace a clinician or pharmacist.

- A caregiver enters medication details directly from the prescription label or a clinician-approved list.
- The app clearly identifies the source as “caregiver-entered plan” and includes a prompt to check the prescription label.
- “Taken” means the member or caregiver marked it as taken; it is not proof of administration or adherence.
- Any change to amount, timing, or active status creates a dated audit record and can optionally notify the household organizer.
- If a member is unsure, has missed a dose, or feels unwell, the app guides them to contact their caregiver, pharmacist, or clinician. It does not tell them what to do with the dose.

## Brand direction

KindCare should feel premium, calm, affectionate, and trustworthy.

- Trust Navy: `#0B4A86`
- Care Teal: `#10C1B5`
- Warm Spark: `#FF724F`
- Soft Mist: `#E8F7F4`
- Ink: `#13233D`
- Cloud: `#F8FBFA`

Use generous whitespace, gentle rounded surfaces, large readable type, and the KindCare hands mark. Avoid generic hospital blue, dense tables, tiny text, and overly cute illustrations.
