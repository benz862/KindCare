# KindCare brand guide

## Brand foundation

**KindCare** is a SkillBinder LLC product for supportive companionship and household care coordination. It should feel calm, capable, human, and respectful—never clinical, infantilizing, or overly sentimental.

The brand promise is simple: help people feel more connected and supported in everyday care.

## Primary tagline

> More connection. More kindness. More peace of mind.

Use the primary tagline in website hero sections, social profiles, launch material, and brand-forward communications. Avoid pairing it with clinical, monitoring, or emergency-service claims.

## Logo

The primary logo is the hands-and-spark mark with the `KindCare` wordmark.

- Asset: `src/assets/brand/kindcare-logo-hands.png`
- Symbol: two open hands gently holding a coral spark.
- Meaning: trusted support, connection, and attentive care.
- Use the complete logo for website headers and social profile covers. Use the hands symbol alone for avatars and app icons.

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the dot over the `i`. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, place on busy photography, or add additional symbols.

## Color palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trust navy | Deep blue | `#0B4A86` |
| Care teal | Bright teal | `#10C1B5` |
| Warm spark | Coral | `#FF724F` |
| Soft mist | Pale teal | `#E8F7F4` |
| Ink | Charcoal navy | `#13233D` |
| Cloud | Off-white | `#F8FBFA` |

Use navy for primary text and authority, teal for positive actions and connection, and coral sparingly for warmth, alerts, or focal accents.

## Typography

- Headings: a warm, highly legible serif such as **Source Serif 4**.
- Interface and body: **Inter** or another clear humanist sans-serif.
- Prioritize large type, generous spacing, strong contrast, and plain language.

## Voice

Write with warmth, clarity, and dignity. Use direct, supportive language such as “You’re in control,” “Talk to someone you trust,” and “Let’s take the next step together.”

Avoid clinical claims, promises of monitoring, emergency-dispatch language, or language that makes the supported person sound passive.

## Ownership and legal language

Use this standard line in footers, support material, and social profiles:

> KindCare is a SkillBinder LLC product.

Use this safety statement where relevant:

> KindCare supports companionship and household care coordination. It is not medical advice, medical monitoring, or emergency dispatch.
